Uruguay might be tiny, but it's truly mighty when it comes to cattle. This South American nation, home to around 3.4 million people, has over 12 million cows grazing its green pastures. That's roughly four cows for every person — one of the highest cattle-to-human ratios in the world!

So, why so many cows? Uruguay's wide grasslands and mild climate make it ideal for livestock. Cattle farming has been the backbone of the economy for centuries, powering its famous beef exports. In fact, Uruguay is one of the world's top beef exporters, sending meat to over 100 countries — all from a country smaller than many Indian states.
